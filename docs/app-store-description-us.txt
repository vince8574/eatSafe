DATA SOURCES
Numeline aggregates public and open data:
- Recalls: the FDA, including its recall press releases, and the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) for meat, poultry, and egg products. Sources: fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts and fsis.usda.gov/recalls
- Product details, ingredients, and allergens: the open Open Food Facts database. Source: world.openfoodfacts.org
None of these sources is affiliated with or endorses Numeline.

BROWSE RECALLS FOR FREE
Browse food recalls published by the FDA and USDA. No scanning, no subscription required.

SCAN YOUR PRODUCTS
Scan a product's barcode, then photograph its lot or batch number: Numeline reads the code and checks, by brand and lot, whether it matches an official FDA or USDA recall. You can also type the lot yourself. Ideal for groceries, inventory, or incoming shipments.

WHEN NO LOT NUMBER IS PUBLISHED
Agencies sometimes announce a recall without publishing lot codes. Numeline then flags a possible recall when the brand and product type match, and shows what was published, such as "Best if Used By" dates, so you can check your item. It also monitors FDA press releases, often published before the enforcement database updates.

ALLERGENS AND DIET
Build a profile and every scan is compared against it:
- The major allergens, including gluten, milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, soybeans, fish, shellfish, sesame, mustard, and sulfites
- Celiac mode for strict gluten avoidance, including traces
- Foods to avoid: pork, seafood, beef, alcohol, gelatin
- Vegetarian and vegan
- Pregnancy mode: raw milk, deli meat, raw fish, meat and eggs, sprouts, high-mercury fish, liver, caffeine
- Thousands of searchable ingredients, plus any food you add yourself
- Thresholds for sugar, fat, saturated fat, and salt
Useful for allergies, intolerances, diets, pregnancy, and diabetes. This information is declarative and may be incomplete. Always check the label.
