Every year in the United States, many food products are recalled. Applications that scan barcodes exist today, but they remain very rudimentary for users, as the burden of manually verifying lot numbers — and therefore the risk of making errors — still falls on their shoulders.

This is why we developed Numeline, an application available on Android and iOS that provides a simple answer to this problem: centralizing official FDA and USDA FSIS data to enable instant consultation via a lot number scan.

This approach transforms access to information — moving from a slow and fragile system to an efficient, fast, and everyday-usable tool. It is an innovation in food safety that concretely aims to improve the prevention of food risks, for both consumers and professionals.

Numeline is also designed for any business working with food products: restaurants, hotels, cinemas, daycares, or any establishment handling or distributing food. I believe this innovation could interest your readers in the context of your publications on public health and new technologies.

I would be delighted to present the application and share additional materials. We can also provide you with trial access so you can test it directly.
